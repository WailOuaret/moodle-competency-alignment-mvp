# UTC Competency Selection for the Moodle MVP

The original UTC framework contains 22 competencies. For the first Moodle
prototype, 13 competencies were selected.

The selection is divided into three coherent branches:

1. GI_C1 — software design and development;
2. GI_C2 — computer architectures, networks and cybersecurity;
3. GI_C3 — data, knowledge representation, databases and artificial intelligence.

This subset was chosen because it:

- contains real IDs from the UTC framework;
- preserves parent-child relationships;
- supports two coherent Moodle test courses;
- contains both clearly different and semantically related competencies;
- provides a meaningful candidate space for BM25;
- allows the LLM to distinguish close competencies;
- remains within the supervisor's requested limit of 10–20 competencies.

Planned Moodle courses:

- Course A: Software Engineering and Distributed Systems;
- Course B: Data, Databases and Intelligent Systems.
